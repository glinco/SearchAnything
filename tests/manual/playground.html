SearchAnything Playground

Every sample below is drawn as a picture, so your browser's own Ctrl+F can't find any of it. Press Ctrl+Alt+F and search with SearchAnything instead. Tick the checklist as you go, write down anything odd, then use Copy report and paste it into the chat.

First, prove the point: press Ctrl+F (the browser's search) and type banana. It finds nothing on these pictures.

1. Accents and capitals

Try accion, configuracion, nino, PINA. All should match.

2. Matches that touch

Try an. Look closely between the boxes: there should be no dark slivers.

3. A phrase split across two lines

Try hola mundo. You should get 1 match with two boxes.

4. Small text

Try tiny. Note which sizes are found. Very small text failing is a known limit, but tell me where it stops working.

5. Colors and contrast

Try contrast. Expect the low-contrast one might be missed.

6. Numbers, symbols, emails

Try 1,240.50, 15/10/2026, user@example.com, A-1234.

7. Many matches

Try test. Hold Enter: the counter should cycle smoothly and wrap from last to first. Try Shift+Enter and F3 too.

8. Moving text (freeze test)

The word keeps moving. Press Ctrl+Alt+F and search moving: the screen should freeze and the box should sit exactly on the word.

9. Rotated text and emoji (expected to fail)

Try rotated and type an emoji like 🚀. These may show "No matches". That's fine; the app just must not break.

10. Top and bottom edges

The bars pinned to the top and bottom of this page hold the words TOPWORD and BOTTOMWORD.

With the browser maximized, search topword: the search bar should jump to the bottom so it doesn't cover the match. Then search bottomword: it should stay at, or come back to, the top.
